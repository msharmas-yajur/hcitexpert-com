⚠ CUTTING GUIDE NOTES — Sheet 7 • Cut sequence: Always cut the longest pieces first (stiles 96", posts 96"), then use remaining stock for shorter rails (72"), then ladder members. This minimises waste from offcuts.
• Mark before cutting: Use chalk to write the piece code (TR, LS, CP, KS etc.) on each piece immediately after cutting. Stack by type. Never cut an unmarked piece.
• Kerf: Each circular saw or mitre saw cut removes ~3mm. For repeatable lengths, clamp a stop-block to the fence rather than measuring each piece individually.
• Mortise pockets: Cut all mortise pockets (2" deep × 1.5" wide × 2" tall) BEFORE assembly using a router and straight bit. Mark positions from the cutting list above. Dry-fit all joints before applying PVA.
• Ply grain direction: For all structural ply panels (TV unit sides, loft shelves), orient grain along the longer dimension for maximum stiffness. Mark grain direction with arrow before ripping.
• Drawer boxes: Cut all drawer sides to identical height using a stop block. Check squareness of each box before glue dries — measure diagonals, they should be equal.

⚠ CARPENTER NOTES — Sheet 7 (LVL Frame Construction) • Assembly order MUST be followed: (1) Fix wall cleats and verify level. (2) Assemble outer frame flat on floor. (3) Laminate and insert centre post. (4) Lift assembled frame onto cleats and bolt. (5) Build ladder sub-frames separately. (6) Drop ladders in, fix with pocket screws. (7) Mount drawer slides. (8) Hang doors. (9) Fit loft shelves and drawers.
• Mortise & tenon spec: Tenon = 1.5" wide × 2" tall × 2" long. Mortise = matching. Use wedged tenons at all four top-rail-to-stile corners (highest stress). PVA structural glue (Fevicol SH). Clamp minimum 24 hours before lifting.
• Ladder sub-frames: Built as a complete ladder unit BEFORE installation. LVL 2"×2" stiles with M&T rungs. Once frame is on wall, the ladder drops into the drawer zone and is fixed with 2 pocket screws top and 2 bottom, from inside the frame (invisible from front). Drawer slides then bolt directly to the ladder stile faces.
• Wall mounting: M10 × 100mm bolts into 12mm masonry plugs. Minimum 3 bolts per cleat, spaced evenly. Torque 25 Nm. All three cleats must be perfectly level with each other — use a spirit level and laser across all three before drilling.
• LVL grade: Minimum E11. Greenlam Sturdo, CenturyPly Club Prime LVL, or ASHA LVL — all available in Bangalore. Specify 50×100mm (2"×4") for main frame and 50×50mm (2"×2") for ladder members.
