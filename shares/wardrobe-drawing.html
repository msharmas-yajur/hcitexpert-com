⚠ CARPENTER NOTES — Sheet 6 (TV Unit) • Zones (bottom to top): 2×16" drawers = 32" · TV recess 42" · AC/loft space 22" = 96" total ✓
• Drawers: Left bank 34" wide + 4"×4" post + right bank 34" wide = 72" total. 2 drawers per side, each 16" high. Soft-close full-extension slides. Handle centred on each drawer.
• 4"×4" Centre Post: Same square timber/ply post as the wardrobes. Sits at the centreline (36" from each side). Only present in the drawer zone (32" high) — does not extend into TV or AC zone.
• TV recess: Designed for 75" TV (~65"W × 37"H screen). No shelf — flush back panel. Cable management cutout at base of recess for HDMI/power. Wall mount bracket fixed through back panel into wall.
• AC space: Open — no doors. Height 22" suits standard cassette or wall-split AC unit. Allow 2–3" air gap at back and sides for ventilation.
• Alignment: TV unit front face flush with both wardrobes. All three units share same 28" depth and same floor line.
• Material: 18mm BWR ply throughout, laminate finish to match wardrobes.
